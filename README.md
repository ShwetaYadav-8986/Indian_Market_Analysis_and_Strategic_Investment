This project explores the Indian financial markets by examining key macroeconomic trends, sector performance, and company strategies. 
It focuses on market volatility and the changing performance of defensive and growth-oriented sectors, while also looking at investment strategies using frameworks such as the BCG Matrix. 
The analysis includes companies like ITC, Adani Green Energy, and Zomato to understand their market position, business strategies, and future growth opportunities.
Overall, the project strengthened my understanding of financial markets, investment approaches, and data-driven decision-making.
